Our Story

Hubble was born from a simple observation: traditional Indian medicine systems, with their rich heritage and proven efficacy, were being left behind in the digital transformation of healthcare. Our team of passionate developers, medical professionals, and AI researchers came together with a shared vision to bridge this gap.

We recognized the critical need for standardized terminology that could connect NAMASTE codes with international classification systems like ICD-11. This wasn't just a technical challenge—it was about preserving the wisdom of traditional medicine while making it accessible in the modern digital healthcare ecosystem.
