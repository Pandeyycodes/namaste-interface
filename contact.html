Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about Hubble

What is Hubble and how does it work?

Hubble is a FHIR-compliant terminology microservice that bridges traditional Indian medicine (NAMASTE codes) with modern healthcare systems (ICD-11). It uses AI-powered mapping algorithms to translate between different medical terminologies, enabling seamless data exchange and interoperability.

Is Hubble compliant with healthcare standards?

Yes, Hubble is fully compliant with FHIR R4 standards and integrates with international classification systems like ICD-11, SNOMED CT, and LOINC. It also follows India's 2016 EHR Standards and supports OAuth 2.0 authentication with ABHA integration.

How accurate are the terminology mappings?

Our AI-powered mapping algorithms achieve over 94% accuracy with confidence scoring. All mappings are validated by medical professionals and continuously improved through machine learning. Low-confidence mappings are flagged for human review.

Can I integrate Hubble with my existing healthcare system?

Absolutely! Hubble provides RESTful APIs and SDKs for easy integration with existing healthcare systems. Our comprehensive documentation and support team will help you implement the integration quickly and efficiently.

What programming languages and platforms are supported?

Hubble supports all major programming languages through our RESTful APIs. We provide SDKs for Python, JavaScript, Java, and C#. The platform is built on FastAPI and can be deployed on any cloud platform or on-premises infrastructure.

Is there a free trial or demo available?

Yes! We offer a free trial with limited API calls and a fully functional demo on our website. You can also request a personalized demo session with our team to see how Hubble can meet your specific requirements.

How is data security and privacy handled?

Data security is our top priority. We use enterprise-grade encryption, follow HIPAA compliance standards, implement OAuth 2.0 authentication, and maintain comprehensive audit trails. All data is processed securely and never shared without explicit consent.

What support options are available?

We provide comprehensive support including documentation, API reference guides, email support, phone support during business hours, and dedicated technical support for enterprise customers. Our team is committed to helping you succeed.
